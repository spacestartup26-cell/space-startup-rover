Behind the game

Four missions.
One red planet.

Our project begins with the stories in Lost Mars Vehicles, our presentation about Mars exploration. From a first rover to missions lost to difficult terrain and dust, each story invites us to ask: what can the next mission learn?

01 1997

Sojourner

Mars Pathfinder

The first rover to operate on Mars showed how a small robot could explore another world.

A small rover. A giant first step.
02 1999

Mars Polar Lander

A mission to the south pole

Designed to study the polar environment, this lander was lost during its arrival at Mars.

Landing is part of the challenge.
03 2004–2010

Spirit

Mars Exploration Rover A

Spirit explored Gusev Crater and found evidence of past water before becoming stuck in soft soil.

The ground can change everything.
04 2004–2018*

Opportunity

Mars Exploration Rover B

“Oppy” explored for more than 14 years. A planet-wide dust storm ended communication in 2018.

Even long journeys face limits.

*Opportunity's final contact was in 2018; NASA declared the mission complete in 2019. Mission summaries checked against NASA Science; references are in the website download.
